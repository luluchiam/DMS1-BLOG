## ASSIGNMENT 1

## How did you approach the relationship between the sonic and visual components of your edit?
I chose a calming song to guide the mood and pace of the visuals. It creates a smooth flow that reflects the peaceful, meaningful aspects of my culture, loved ones, and daily life.

## How did you decide on the footage that you used to shape your project?
I chose footage that best represented my positive energy and what/who I care about most. Since the project was about our self portrait, I wanted to show the highlights of my recent years. 

## What did you learn about the power of sound to shape emotional experience, and how did you apply this knowledge to your project?
I learned that sound has a strong influence on emotions and can set the tone for a piece. By choosing a calming song, I wanted to create a peaceful, reflective mood in my self-portrait video. 

## How does editing relate to the concepts of pacing and rhythm, and how did you use these concepts to shape your use of time in the project?
I used smooth transitions to match the calming music and create a relaxed pace. This helped the video feel more reflective and show the emotions I wanted to express.

## Which parts of your personality, interests, past, and potential future did you choose to depict in your portrait?
I chose to show parts of my personality through my connection to my culture, my close relationships with friends and family, and the activities I love doing every day. These elements reflect who I am in the present, my values, and the things that bring me joy. 

## What were the primary aesthetic choices that you made to express those ideas in your work?
I decided to go with a gentle pace to reflect the relaxed, everyday moments with my friends, family, and the things I love to do. 

## ASSIGNMENT 2

## How would you summarise your idea behind the suite of icons, and why did you choose the theme that you did? 
The idea behind this suite of icons revolves around themes of dreams. Each icon represents a different element of the dream world such as sleep, thoughts, time, and vision capturing the abstract and symbolic nature of dreams. I chose this theme because it aligns with the concept of exploring the subconscious, creativity, and the passage of time, all of which are important aspects of how we interpret and experience dreams.

## Did you iterate through a series of approaches before settling on one? What changed across those iterations, and why did you choose the one that you ended up with?
I did have different ideas before settling on one but since the beginning of this project I knew I wanted to keep the icons simple and straightforward so that is why at the end all my icons are minimalist yet they definitely expressed what I was trying to show which was the theme of dream. 

## How did you translate your thumbnail sketches into the final flat designs in Illustrator? Was anything lost in that translation, or gained?
I used my thumbnail sketches as a guide to create the final flat designs in Illustrator by refining the shapes and lines, keeping the overall concepts the same. In the process, I simplified some details to fit the clean, minimalist style I wanted. Nothing important was lost, but I gained a more polished and cohesive look that worked well as a set of icons.

## How did you use colour to create a sense of unity and visual interest in the icons? 
I used analogous colours, such as blues and purples to create a sense of unity across the icons. This choice not only enhances visual interest but also evokes a calming and dreamy atmosphere that aligns with the theme. 

## Why did you choose the palette that you did?
I chose analogous colours like blue and purple because they create a serene and cohesive look that reflects the theme of dreams. 

## How did the elements and principles of sound that you used in your audio design correspond to the elements and principles of design that you used in your visual design?
The audio and visual elements work together to evoke a dreamy atmosphere, enhancing the overall experience and reinforcing the theme of dreams. The audio that was chosen for each icon matched really well and could give the viewers a satisfying reaction. 

## How did you translate the prompt that you chose from an abstract idea to a set of concrete visual designs?
I translated the abstract idea from the prompt into concrete visual designs by focusing on key symbols that represent the themes. I identified simple, recognisable elements like stars and clouds that visually conveyed concepts of dreams. Then, I turned these symbols into clean, minimal icons to create a clear, cohesive visual representation of the prompt. 

## ASSIGNMENT 3

## How important was a working prototype to your design process? What did it offer that process?
A working prototype was important to my design process because it allowed me to test and visualise my ideas in real-time. It helped identify potential issues early so that I could make improvements.

## How did your experience of designing and producing a three-dimensional environment differ from your experience of designing and producing a project in two-dimensional media? 
Producing a three-dimensional environment required considering depth, scale, and user interaction in a more immersive way. Unlike 2D, I had to think about how users would move through and experience the space, which added complexity and required more attention.

## What was the role of the virtual camera in your project, and how was it similar to and different from the role of the camera in Assignment 1?
In my 3D environment, the virtual camera played a key role in controlling the viewer’s perspective and guiding their movement through the space. This was similar to Assignment 1 where the camera also directed focus and framed important details. However, in the 3D environment, the virtual camera allowed for interactive navigation, whereas in the video, the camera captured a fixed sequence.

## How did you use sound in your project, and how was this similar to and different from your use of sound in Assignments 1 and 2?
In my 3D environment project, I used sound to enhance immersion and guide user experience, creating a more realistic atmosphere. This was similar to my self-portrait video, where sound also set the mood and reflected personal themes. The 3D project required careful placement and layering of sounds to interact naturally with the environment as users navigated through it.

## How did you unify the different elements of your virtual environment under a certain theme or concept? How did your theme or concept evolve over the project’s development?
I unified the elements of my virtual environment by using a consistent colour palette that fit my theme. Initially, my theme focused on personal growth, but as the project evolved, I added natural elements like soft lighting to emphasise a calm, grounded atmosphere, symbolising inner peace.

## What did you discover through the process of play testing that you wouldn’t have discovered otherwise?
Through playtesting, I discovered how users interacted with and moved through the environment differently than I’d expected. This feedback revealed areas where navigation could be improved and where adjustments were needed to make the experience smoother.
